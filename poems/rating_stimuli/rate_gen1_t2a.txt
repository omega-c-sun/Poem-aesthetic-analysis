The streetlamp hums its thin and jaundiced song,
A weary chorus to the sleeping stone.
The city's breath is shallow, held too long,
And I am utterly, and quite alone.
My footsteps make a lonely, ticking sound
That bounces off the glass and fades to null,
As if the dark were swallowing the ground
On which I walk, a pathway rendered null.
A single window glows a sulfur square,
A distant ship upon a sea of black.
I do not know the soul who kindles there,
Nor does he know the ghost that wanders back.
So I will keep this vigil, cold and deep,
And hold my solitude till morning's keep.
