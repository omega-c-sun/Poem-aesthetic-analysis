The streetlight hums a pale and constant note,
The asphalt gleams with rain that fell at noon.
A single window, high above, afloat,
Holds back the dark with one unsteady moon.
The traffic’s distant pulse, a muted drum,
Beats time for no one walking here but me.
The empty benches wait for hours to come,
And storefront glass repeats what I can’t see.
This solitude is not a thing to fear,
But like a room where echoes learn their name;
The city breathes, and I am merely here,
A leaf that drifted from a hidden flame.
So let the night unspool its silent thread—
I’ll walk until the stars have gone to bed.
