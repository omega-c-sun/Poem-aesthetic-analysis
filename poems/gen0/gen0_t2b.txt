The city breathes in iron lung at night,
And windows gleam like eyes that do not sleep;
The asphalt rivers hold the lonely light,
Where shadows crawl and tangled sorrows creep.
I walk a canyon made of stone and steel,
My footsteps echo where no footstep stays,
A pulse I feel but never can reveal,
Lost in the maze of these electric days.
But then—a sudden flare, a taxi’s brake!
The world lurches and shatters my still trance;
A drunkard’s laugh, a siren’s wail awake
The ghost of motion in this vast expanse.
Yet no hand reaches, no voice calls my name—
I am the echo, and the echo’s shame.
