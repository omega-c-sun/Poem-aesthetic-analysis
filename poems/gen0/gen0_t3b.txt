The mind is not a vessel, but a tide
That turns upon a shore of vanished years;
It coils the kelp of moments, set aside
In briny hollows, salt with unshed tears.
The drowned town’s bell still tolls beneath the deep,
A muffled chime the current bends and braids;
The faces that we loved in silence sleep
Where coral builds her patient, cold arcades.
But wake!—this flood is not a grave, but glass
That shatters on the keel of a new sun!
What once was drowned, the living waves surpass—
The sea that took, the sea returns as one.
So let the brine recast what time unwove:
The tide that lost you is the tide I love.
