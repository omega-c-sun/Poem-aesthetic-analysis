When generating sonnets, prioritize a strong, consistent central image or symbol that threads through all quatrains and the couplet. Use verbs that convey physical struggle or transformation rather than passive states. Vary sentence structure and rhythm within the iambic pentameter to avoid monotony, employing enjambment, caesura, and internal sound devices. Ensure rhymes are fresh and avoid near-identical or clichéd pairs. The volta should emerge from a shift in imagery or tone, not from explicit conjunctions. Aim for concrete, sensory language that evokes touch, sound, and movement, and avoid abstract statements unless immediately grounded in a physical metaphor.
